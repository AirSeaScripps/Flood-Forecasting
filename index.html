Total water level forecasts from waves (CDIP MOP) and water levels (NOAA CO-OPS), with probabilities from an ensemble of the model's uncertainties. "Highest" is the chance in the single most likely hour; "at least one tide" is the chance over the whole forecast. Drain backflow is flagged where a storm drain inlet on a network without a tide gate sits below the forecast still water level. Rain is the NOAA National Blend of Models forecast averaged over the site; "most drains closed" means at least half of the bay-connected inlets are closed by the still water level, and compound flooding is flagged for rain of 2.5 mm/h or more at those times. Times are local to each site. Data: latest.json · history.csv · GeoJSON. Made with TRIM_Storm .
